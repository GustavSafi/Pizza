PIZZERIA WEBSITE MASTER PLAN (MODERN ITALIAN STYLE)

1. GOAL & VISION
- Sell pizza online (delivery/takeaway)
- Build strong Italian premium brand
- Showcase menu and experience
- Drive traffic via SEO

2. TARGET AUDIENCE
- Local customers
- Food enthusiasts
- Tourists (mobile-first users)

3. DESIGN SYSTEM
Colors:
- Dark Green (#1F3D2B)
- Wine Red (#7A1E1E)
- Beige (#F5E6D3)
- Near Black (#1A1A1A)

Typography:
- Headings: Serif (Playfair Display)
- Body: Sans-serif (Inter)
- Accent: Handwritten style

UI:
- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Glassmorphism overlays
- Subtle hover animations
